Moon texture assets
===================

- `lroc_color_2k.jpg`: NASA's 2025 LROC color map from the CGI Moon Kit. It is
  adapted from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Wide Angle Camera mosaic.
- `ldem_3_8bit.jpg`: NASA's LOLA displacement map from the CGI Moon Kit.

Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio; Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter
Camera and Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter instrument teams.
